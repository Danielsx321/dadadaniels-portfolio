## The problem

Caitlin sells digital products on Etsy and shares the real numbers on YouTube. She had a moodboard, a brand partnership deck and a draft homepage, but no website: nowhere to send viewers, collect emails, sell her own guides or give brands a place to be featured.

## What I did

- **Design:** turned the moodboard and deck into a site-wide design system (palette, three fonts, arch photo frames, brush underlines) and cut her wordmark from the deck as the logo. Built on Kadence and the block editor, so there's no page builder and no yearly license.
- **Pages:** Home, Start Here (three stages, each with its own free guide), About, a filterable blog, Free Resources, Contact, and a privacy policy and terms written for Australian rules.
- **Shop:** WooCommerce for her paid guides, with Stripe (cards and Klarna), GST-inclusive AUD pricing and protected downloads that go out by email the moment payment clears.
- **Email:** Kit signup forms wired straight to Kit, no plugin, each delivering its free guide. Moved sending to her own domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC passing, so signup and order emails land in the inbox.
- **Tools page:** her affiliate and sponsor links as cards with logos, plus a Featured row that styles any card moved into it, so she can sell featured placement to brands without touching code.
- **Tracking and speed:** GA4 with shop events, so every sale shows as a conversion with its value. Swapped the YouTube embeds for click-to-play thumbnails to cut the page weight.
- **Handover:** her own admin account and a written guide for running the site day to day.

## Result

The site launched on 28 September and the first sales came in two days later. Swapping the video embeds cut the mobile home page from **1,667 KiB to 711 KiB** and blocking time from **320 ms to 120 ms**. Caitlin approved every milestone, left a 5 star review, and runs the site herself now.
