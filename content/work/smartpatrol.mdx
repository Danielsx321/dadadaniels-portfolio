## The problem

Smart Patrol is the shipping innovation program of Rijkswaterstaat, the Dutch government agency for roads and waterways. As a government website it has to meet strict accessibility rules, and the site needed work to get there.

## What I did

I handled the accessibility side of an existing WordPress and Elementor Pro site. I did not build the site itself.

- Worked through pages, popups and the filterable project listings so they can be used with a keyboard and a screen reader.
- Covered the basics that are easy to miss on a big site: a skip-to-content link, descriptive image alt text and accessible popups.

## Result

The PageSpeed Insights accessibility score reached **100**.
