## The problem

Chabad of Frisco runs a yearly community Chanukah concert at The Star in Frisco, Texas. They needed a site that sells registrations, brings in sponsors, and can be updated for next year's concert without hiring a developer again.

## What I built

- A custom WordPress theme in the navy and gold of the event flyer, with a live countdown, moving stage lights, this year's headliner, a scroll timeline of past years and a photo gallery.
- Register buttons that open Eventbrite checkout on the site itself, falling back to the Eventbrite page if needed.
- A Sponsors page with sponsorship levels, a proposal PDF button and a presenting sponsors strip on every page, plus Location and Parking and Contact pages.
- Everything that changes each year lives in the Customizer and a Timeline Years list, with a written update guide.
- Fixed form emails that Gmail was silently dropping: every entry is now saved in WordPress first and sent through the client's own Gmail.
- Connected the new domain with SSL on both versions of the address and checked every page asset on it.

## Result

The site was delivered on chanukahinfrisco.com on 26 October 2026, ahead of the 6 December concert, after two rounds of client edits.
