## The problem

Crown Fulfillment is a 3PL warehouse in Elizabeth, New Jersey. Its site was custom-coded on Replit, so every change needed a developer. The owner wanted a WordPress site the team could edit, with working quote forms, and a move that would not touch the company email.

## What I built

- Rebuilt all 12 pages on WordPress in the same look, so the owner can edit every word without a developer.
- Four Forminator forms with branded inquiry emails sent through Brevo, with SPF and DKIM aligned and the domain's DMARC quarantine policy kept.
- Calendly booking straight after a quote is sent, and a two-step Free Move landing page with a countdown and a Wistia video.
- A port proximity map, per-page titles, LocalBusiness schema and a sitemap.
- Moved the site live by changing one DNS record, leaving the mail records alone.
- Follow-on: five service pages built from the owner's copy, clickable service cards, a sitewide promo bar, and a 2.1 MB hero GIF swapped for a looping video under 100 KB.

## Result

The site is live on usethecrown.com, the owner edits it without a developer, and email never went down during the move.
