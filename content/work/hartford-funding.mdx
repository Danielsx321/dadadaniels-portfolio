## The problem

Hartford Funding, a mortgage lender, needed Google Ads landing pages that matched its designs, fed leads straight to loan officers and could count as conversions. The first mockup's HTML would not render in WPBakery at all: styling printed as text, the layout broke and the form was not connected.

## What I built

- **HELOC landing page:** rebuilt in WPBakery the way the lender's own pages are built, matched to the mockup on desktop and phone, with a short lead form sent to two loan officers and the lender's compliance footnotes.
- **Confirmation page:** the loan officer's Calendly embedded and prefilled from the form, without putting personal data in the URL, so Google Ads rules are kept.
- **2-1 buydown calculator:** a live calculator for loan amount, term and rate showing buydown cost, Year 1, Year 2 and full-rate payments, with a lead form that carries the loan amount over.
- **DSCR landing page:** built from the client's own design with a three-step quote form, a DSCR calculator and three headline variants for the ad groups, every lead saved in WordPress with the ad source and click ID.
- Installed the IT team's Tag Manager container on the landing and confirmation pages without plugin or theme access, and verified it fires.

## Result

All three pages are live and taking leads. Stats and testimonial sections on the DSCR page stay hidden until the lender has real figures, rather than shipping made-up ones.
