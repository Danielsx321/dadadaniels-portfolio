## The problem

CPHnights organizes nights out for university students in Copenhagen. They needed one place where students can find events and buy tickets, and where the team can promote each night.

## What I built

A dark, high energy event website on WordPress and Elementor that showcases their Night Pass and VIP experiences, lists upcoming events and takes ticket purchases.
