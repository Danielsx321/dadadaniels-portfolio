## The problem

Richard Saad runs workshops and talks for corporate teams planning off-sites and strategy sessions. Event planners in Montreal work in English and French, and they need to see what each workshop covers and book a call quickly.

## What I built

A WordPress and Elementor site, fully bilingual in English and French.

- **Five workshop pages** on one clear template: who it's for, learning goals, session topics, formats and price ranges.
- **A Book a call button** on every workshop page, linked to Calendly.
- Automatic language detection with a switch prompt, a contact form and SEO setup.
