## The problem

SMG Worldwide Relief Network delivers disaster relief and community support. They needed a website that does two jobs well: take donations and sign up volunteers, without either one feeling like an afterthought.

## What I built

I built the site on WordPress with Elementor Pro, around those two jobs.

- **Online donations** with preset amounts ($10, $20, $50, $100) or a custom amount, plus proper confirmation and failed payment pages so no donor is left guessing.
- **Volunteer sign-up** that verifies each phone number with a one-time code, so the team only gets genuine sign-ups.
- **Cause pages and a news section** for relief updates, including their work during the January 2025 Los Angeles wildfires.
- **An enquiry form**, GDPR and CCPA cookie consent, a privacy page, and a custom header and footer that work on every screen size.
