## The problem

Window Solutions is a window tinting business with three shops on Florida's east coast, in Sebastian, Satellite Beach and Fort Pierce, running two Elementor sites on Bluehost. The Sebastian site was telling Google it was the Satellite Beach shop: the wrong business name, phone number and logo in its structured data, and the other shop's number in the header. The Satellite Beach site carried Sebastian's phone and a placeholder 555 number. Nine pages had no H1, dead pages and temporary-domain links were still live, and the three Google Business Profiles were thin.

## What I did

- Fixed both sites so each one's business data matches its own Google Business Profile, removed the temporary-domain links, dead pages and test posts, and added a single H1 to the nine pages that had none.
- On the three Business Profiles: rewrote a four-word description, added a second category, linked Instagram and set correct hours. Checked every major directory for all three shops and mapped the gaps.
- Set a Search Console and mobile PageSpeed baseline per site (three months of clicks and positions) so every later milestone is measured against it.
- Rebuilt the header and footer on both sites around the layout the owner picked from a competitor: call and Book Now buttons, a service row, live Google reviews on every page, the address linked to Maps, and a call-or-book bar that stays on screen on phones.
- Split each site's combined home and commercial page into separate Residential and Commercial pages with local copy and a 301 from the old address.
- Found the hero photos loading late because they sat on a scroll-effect layer, so phones now fetch a smaller copy first. Then a site-wide stylesheet the owner can switch off: one hero pattern, readable text width, one button and link style, and the brand blue darkened so white text passes contrast (it was 2.97 to 1).

## Result

Sebastian homepage mobile PageSpeed went from **64 to 76** and the hero appears at 4.6 seconds instead of 7.2. Satellite Beach desktop went from **91 to 97** on the homepage. Each site now sends Google its own shop's name, phone and logo, with the Business Profiles to match. Work continues through further milestones.
