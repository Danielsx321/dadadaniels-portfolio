## The problem

PeoplePlacesThingsMiami covers Miami's nightlife, music, art and community, and sells placement to event organizers. The events side of the publication is where organizers and readers meet, so it has to be easy for both.

## What I built

I built everything event related on the site. The rest of the publication was not my work.

- **Event submissions:** a Submit Event call to action in the header that takes organizers through a step by step submission for editorial or paid placement.
- **Event listings and guides:** how events show up for readers, including the weekly Miami Weekend Navigator.
