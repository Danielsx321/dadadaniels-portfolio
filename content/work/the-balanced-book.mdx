## The problem

The Balanced Book is a Dallas bookkeeping and tax prep firm for small businesses and new founders. The brief was an accounting website that doesn't feel like one.

## What I built

A Squarespace site with a bold, playful layout and an animated homepage marquee, clear pages for monthly bookkeeping, tax filing prep and custom bundles, and a single booking flow so every enquiry lands in one place. The contact form is spam protected and the store is ready for the firm's downloadable guides.
