## The problem

Core Wellness Institute runs health education memberships for about 1,100 members on WishList Member, across three WordPress sites on one VPS. A hosting move had left one site offline for seven weeks, and the main site was stuck on an abandoned theme.

## What I did

- Brought the offline site back: files and database moved to the VPS, nginx and PHP set up, self-renewing SSL, Cloudflare DNS switched, and the main site's dead Join link working again.
- Moved the main site to a Kadence child theme, staging first: all 11 old page layouts mapped, the 26 header-free sales pages kept as they were, and menus, sidebars and member messages recreated.
- Put a member login at the top of the phone layout after the owner's review.
- Compared all 456 published pages old against new (status, text, images, video, forms, errors) on staging and again on live, and tested member access across every level.
- Blocked backup files that could be downloaded from two of the sites.

## Result

**Zero differences across 456 pages** after the switch, and member access matched on both themes. The owner's next request is the same move for a second site.
