## The problem

Event organizers wanted to list their events and sell tickets straight to their audience, instead of paying a third-party ticketing service on every sale.

## What I built

A responsive event ticketing website on WordPress and Elementor, with event listings that are easy to browse, a secure checkout and automated invoicing, so organizers keep control of their sales.
