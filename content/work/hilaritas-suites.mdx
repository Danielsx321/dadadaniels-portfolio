## The problem

Hilaritas Suites rents two boutique suites, Livie and Irida, in the village of Ratzakli on Kefalonia. The owners wanted guests to find them, see the suites properly and enquire directly, in English and in Greek.

## What I did

Built the site on WordPress with every page in English and Greek behind an EN / GR switcher. The homepage opens on a full-screen photo slider. Each suite has its own page with occupancy, size, view, design style and the full amenity list, and there is a gallery, a location page and pages for experiences and tailored services.

Guest reviews come in live from Booking.com, and the contact page has an enquiry form with GDPR consent, click-to-call, email and a map. Images lazy load through LiteSpeed so the photo-heavy pages stay quick.

In 2026 the owners came back for WCAG accessibility fixes and the EU funding notice their ESPA grant requires, both added to the live site.
