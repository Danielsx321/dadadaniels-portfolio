## The problem

A luxury and exotic car parts retailer in Winston-Salem, North Carolina needed a store that could sell priced parts straight away and still take quote requests for the rare ones. Buyers needed to know a part fits before they ask, and many pay by bank transfer, not card.

## What I built

- **Brand and store:** a black, white and red design from the brand files, a custom icon set, and header, phone header and footer rebuilt from the Mobex auto parts theme with the clutter removed. Every custom change lives in a child theme, so theme updates stay safe.
- **Fitment:** a Year, Make, Model, Trim and Engine filter on the homepage, a Shop by Vehicle page and the shop sidebar, plus a VIN lookup that maps each decoded VIN onto the store's own vehicle list.
- **Verify Fitment on every product:** fits, trim to confirm, or not listed, with a one-click quote when the answer is not a clear yes.
- **Buy or quote:** priced products go through the cart, products without a price show Request a Quote automatically. Four request forms (quote, part, special order, custom build) take file uploads, save to the dashboard with a status and reply straight to the customer.
- **Payments and shipping:** ACH and wire transfer with orders on hold and the order number as the reference, shipping zones with four classes and freight flags, shipment tracking with a branded Shipped email.
- **Launch:** a branded coming-soon gate with a client preview link, made safe for the host's cache, then About, Contact and seven policy pages before the store went public.

## Result

The store went live on 12 October 2026 after both milestones were approved. The client then hired me back to load an 80 product catalog.
