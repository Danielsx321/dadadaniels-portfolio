## The problem

Goali's Gourmet sells handmade dog chips. No US address was offered a shipping option, so no one could check out, and the last shipped order was months old. A security scan also showed critical findings left over from a 2023 break-in.

## What I did

- Proved the checkout fault from outside with cart tests on several US addresses, then found the cause: shipping came only from two outdated live-rate plugins with no fallback. Set up a US flat rate with free shipping over a threshold and tested checkout.
- Put the owner's real product photos first on the chicken listings, converted and compressed.
- Removed the 10,614 spam posts (restoring the one real article caught up with them), the planted files and old leftovers, regenerated security keys and reset admin access.
- Removed 11 unused or vulnerable plugins after rebuilding the homepage banner and product grid in free Elementor, and fixed a missing product page header and blank phone menu icons.
- Opened the product pages to Google with a product sitemap, after finding every product set to noindex.
- Retested pages, cart, shipping and checkout logged out on desktop and phone.

## Result

**US customers can check out again**, the spam is gone from the site and the sitemap, and the theme's daily fatal error stopped once the cleanup was done.
